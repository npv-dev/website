title: "Privacy"
---

This statement is made under the Privacy Act 2020, information privacy principle 3 (IPP 3), which requires us to tell you what we collect and why.

**What we collect.** If you email hello@npv.co.nz, we collect your email address, your name if you give it, and the contents of your message. This website uses no cookies, tracking or analytics, and it has no forms.

**Why we collect it.** To read and reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of our business correspondence.

**Who receives it.** NPV Development Limited receives your message. Email is delivered through Google Workspace. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. We also use document-storage and AI processing service providers to keep and manage our business records. All of these act on our behalf as our service providers. We do not sell personal information.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@npv.co.nz. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz/).
